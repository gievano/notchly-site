the notch
windows never had.

music with synced lyrics, calendar, file tray, focus timer and todo —
tucked into the top of your screen.

download for windows view on github

free & open source (MIT) · portable exe, no install

one notch, five tabs

now playing

title, artist, cover art and controls, straight from the windows SMTC api. works with spotify, browsers, mpv — anything in the volume flyout. click the progress bar to seek.

synced lyrics

lyrics fetched from lrclib.net with the current line highlighted as the song plays. looked up once, cached locally, instant the next time.

calendar

a month view with today highlighted. no account, no sync, just the date.

shelf

a file tray for the things you need within reach. drag files in from explorer, click to open them later.

focus

25 / 5 / 15 minute timer with a daily session count. while a session runs, the countdown lives in the collapsed notch.

todo

a plain local task list. check things off, delete them, move on.

built to disappear

one window, one small watcher process, no background services. the media watcher polls once a second while music plays and slows down when nothing does. the lyrics cache caps itself. it sits at the top of your screen and stays out of your way.

get notchly

grab the portable exe from the releases page and run it. that's the whole install.

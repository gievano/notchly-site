notchly is a mac-style notch for windows. it shows what’s playing with synced lyrics, plus a calendar, a file tray, a focus timer and a todo list — in 30 pixels at the top of your screen. the notch up there is the real demo: hover it, like the actual app.

download for windows

free · open source (MIT) · portable exe, no install

now playing

title, artist, cover and controls from the windows SMTC api. works with spotify, browsers, mpv — anything in the volume flyout. click the progress bar to seek.

synced lyrics

from lrclib.net, highlighted line by line as the song plays. looked up once, cached locally, instant the next time.

calendar

month view. today is highlighted. that’s it.

shelf

drag files in from explorer. click to open them later.

focus

25 / 5 / 15 minute timer with a daily count. while a session runs, the countdown lives in the notch.

todo

a local list. check things off.

light

one window. one small watcher process. it polls once a second while music plays, every three seconds when idle. the lyrics cache caps itself at 200 songs. nothing runs at startup unless you ask it to.

get it

one file, no installer.
